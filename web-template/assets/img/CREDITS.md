## Replacing with your own photography

Drop your own JPG/WebP/AVIF images at the same paths and the layout will
absorb them. Recommended specs:

- Hero & class photos: 1600 × 2000px (4:5) or 1600 × 1067px (3:2), AVIF/WebP, ≤ 250kb
- Instructor portraits: 600 × 750px (4:5), ≤ 100kb
- Studio interior: 1600 × 900px (16:9), AVIF/WebP, ≤ 250kb
